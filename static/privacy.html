Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What the extension reads

Only the web address of the tab you click it on, and only at the moment you click. It uses Chrome's activeTab permission, so it can't see your other tabs, your browsing history or anything you type.

What it sends

  • The store's address is sent to this website's inspector, which visits the store's public pages (the page itself, /meta.json and /products.json) to build the report.
  • When you click Get AI tips, the report is sent to Google Gemini to write the tips.

What is stored

Nothing. Reports aren't saved, there are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. To stop abuse, the server briefly counts requests per visitor (for the hourly limit) and forgets them after an hour.
